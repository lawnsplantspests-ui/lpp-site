What Pests Harrisburg Actually Deals With

Harrisburg pest issues split sharply by neighborhood. City row homes in Midtown, Allison Hill, and Uptown deal with ants, spiders, and overwintering pests — pressure driven by shared walls, older infrastructure, and proximity to the Susquehanna riverfront. Inner suburbs (Penbrook, Paxtang, Bellevue Park) see more ants, spiders, and ground-nesting yellow jackets in mature landscaping. Outer suburbs (Colonial Park, Lower Paxton, Susquehanna Township) get carpenter bees, cluster flies, and stinging insects in soffits.

We treat all three patterns — same company, different approach. The person who answers the phone is the same licensed PA applicator who walks your property, so the diagnosis matches the treatment.

Pests We Handle in Harrisburg

Ants

Odorous house ants, pavement ants, carpenter ants. Bait-first approach so the colony, not just the foragers, takes the hit. Particularly common in the inner suburbs every spring.

Yellow Jackets & Hornets

Ground-nesting yellow jackets, bald-faced hornets, European hornets, paper wasps. Same-day emergency response for active aerial nests near foot traffic. Dedicated yellow jacket service page.

Spiders

Web removal plus targeted perimeter treatment. Most common: cellar spiders, common house spiders, the seasonal wolf spider in the fall. We identify before treating.

Seasonal Prevention

A monthly perimeter program for households that want pests caught at the door instead of treated after they’re inside (every other month and quarterly also available) — first-floor windows and doors, plus three feet up the wall and three feet out from the foundation all the way round the house, every month. Every other visit also gets a granular application through the mulch and rock beds, where ants and other crawling insects harbour. This is how most of our customers are set up, and it is the approach that actually works on older Harrisburg homes with multiple entry points.

What We Don't Treat Rodent control (mice and rats) and cockroach extermination aren't part of our service mix. For either, reach out to a specialist focused on those pests. Squirrels, raccoons, groundhogs, and other nuisance wildlife are handled by our wildlife removal service.

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm in a row home — does pest control even work when my neighbors aren't treating?
Partially. We do exterior perimeter treatment around your unit — foundation, doorframes, window penetrations, soffits — which stops most ants from getting in from outside. If they are already inside, expect a few treatments to get control rather than one: the colony is outdoors and it takes repeat contact to knock it back. See pest control, not extermination. But if your neighbor's unit has an active interior infestation that's pushing through shared walls, we can't address that side. We're up front about what perimeter work can and can't do. Note: we don't treat roaches or rodents at all — for those you'll need a specialist.
What does pest control cost in Harrisburg?
Single-treatment costs run $100–$250 depending on the pest and property. The monthly perimeter program runs $70–$100 a month, March through November. Every other month and quarterly are available as well; monthly is what most customers are on. City row homes often run less than suburban houses just because there's less perimeter. Stinging insect nests run $165–$225 for ground nests, more for soffit and aerial. Free estimates by phone.
Can you come tonight for a yellow jacket nest?
Often yes — we hold same-day emergency slots through summer for active stinging insect nests, especially with kids or allergic family members nearby. Call as early as you can. Nests are treated during normal working hours — dust puffed into the nest entrance — so you're not waiting until after dark.
Do you handle commercial pest control?
Yes. Restaurants, offices, retail, and small light-industrial. We hold the PA license categories needed for commercial work and carry liability appropriate for commercial settings.
