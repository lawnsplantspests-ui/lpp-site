What Hershey Properties Actually Deal With

The pest mix in Hershey reflects the neighborhood. Estate lots in Derry Township get wildlife-adjacent issues: carpenter ants in the eaves, yellow jackets in ground voids near patio stone work, cluster flies that overwinter in sun-warmed south walls, and carpenter bees in cedar fascia. Newer subdivisions off Sand Beach and Bullfrog Valley get the standard ant/spider/wasp pressure plus the chronic odorous house ants that come from new-construction soil disturbance.

Properties along the Hersheypark perimeter and Spring Creek also deal with more pressure from the woodline year-round — carpenter ants moving in from rotting limbs, yellow jackets in stone walls, and ground-nesting hornets near deck stonework. Different from open-lot pest pressure two miles away.

Pests We Handle in Hershey

Ants

Odorous house ants, carpenter ants, pavement ants, and the occasional acrobat ant. We identify the species first — different ants need different products and different bait strategies. If ants are already coming indoors, expect a few treatments to get control rather than one; the colony is outside and it takes repeat contact. See pest control, not extermination. Carpenter ant activity in mature Derry Township homes often indicates a moisture issue we'll flag.

Yellow Jackets & Hornets

Ground-nesting yellow jackets in patios and stone walls, bald-faced hornets in shrubs and soffits, European hornets in tree cavities. Same-day response for active nests with kids or pets nearby. Dedicated yellow jacket service page.

Spiders

Web removal plus targeted treatment around foundation, eaves, and outbuildings. Brown recluse is rare in Central PA but we identify and handle if found.

Carpenter Bees

The chronic spring problem for Hershey cedar fascia, deck railings, and unpainted soffits. Treatment targets the galleries; long-term fix involves repainting or repairing.

Seasonal Prevention

A monthly perimeter program for households that want pests handled before they show up indoors (every other month and quarterly also available) — first-floor windows and doors, plus three feet up the wall and three feet out from the foundation all the way round the house, every month. Every other visit also gets a granular application through the mulch and rock beds, where ants and other crawling insects harbour. This is how most of our customers are set up, and it is especially useful on Hershey estate lots with multiple outbuildings.

What We Don't Treat Rodent control (mice and rats) and cockroach extermination aren't part of our service mix. For either, reach out to a specialist focused on those pests. Squirrels, raccoons, groundhogs, and other nuisance wildlife are handled separately under nuisance wildlife.

Frequently Asked Questions

I have a yellow jacket nest in my deck — can you come today?
Usually yes. We hold same-day slots through summer specifically for active stinging insect emergencies — especially for households with kids, pets, or anyone allergic. Call as early in the day as possible. Nests are treated during normal working hours — dust puffed into the nest entrance — so you're not waiting until after dark.
What does pest control cost in Hershey?
Single-treatment costs run $100–$250 depending on the pest and property. The monthly perimeter program runs $70–$100 a month, March through November, depending on the size of the house. Every other month and quarterly are available as well; monthly is what most customers are on. Stinging insect nests run $165–$225 for ground nests, more for soffit and aerial nests. Free estimates by phone.
My ant problem comes back every spring — why?
Most likely you have a nearby colony that the original treatment didn't fully reach. Hershey's mature landscapes are full of mulch beds, decorative stone, and retaining walls that hide satellite colonies. We approach recurring ant problems with bait first (lets the workers carry the active back to the colony) instead of perimeter spray, which only knocks down foragers.
Do you handle honey bees?
Yes — and the goal is always to get them out alive, through our partner apiary work. Live removal is our policy and what we always try first. It depends on the colony being reachable safely and the removal being cost-effective for you — where it isn't, we'll tell you that up front and talk through the options rather than surprise you. See honey bee removal.
