Why "Pollinator Restoration" Is a Real Service

We also operate The Alleman Apiary, which means bees from our own apiaries forage on the same properties we treat. If we cut corners on pollinator protection, we're poisoning our own bees. So pollinator-aware practices aren't a marketing layer for us — they're operational necessity.

Pollinator-Friendly Lawn Programs

Bloom-aware timing, reduced-risk product choices, tolerance for clover and other pollinator-supporting plants, spot treatments instead of broadcast spraying. A healthy lawn that also functions as pollinator habitat.

Pesticide Audit & Modification

We'll review what's being applied on your property, when, and where. We identify neonicotinoid use on flowering plants, foliar sprays during bloom, and broadcast spraying that hits pollinators — and tell you what to do about it.

Pollinator Strip Installation & Native Plant Guidance

For unused space — fence lines, orchard edges, field margins — we help you establish native flowering pollinator strips that bloom across the season. Species recommendations specific to Central PA conditions.

Bee-Safe Mosquito Control

In2Care targeted stations and EcoGuard Plus organic barrier treatment — alternatives to broadcast spraying that kill mosquitoes without wiping out the bees, butterflies, and fireflies in your yard.

Beneficial Nematodes (Living Biological Pest Control)

Chiefly for root weevils and borer larvae on ornamentals — and also grubs, sod webworms, fleas and ticks — we offer beneficial nematode applications as a living, soil-applied alternative to synthetic insecticides. No pollinator impact, no residual chemistry, no harm to earthworms or soil life. The default insect-control option for properties where pollinator protection is the goal. Frequently Asked Questions

Will pollinator-friendly lawn care leave my yard looking weedy?
Not if it's done right. A healthy lawn outcompetes weeds — the issue isn't pollinator-friendly products, it's lawn health. We can produce a lawn that looks great AND supports pollinators.
Do I have to go fully organic?
No. There's a spectrum. Fully organic is one option. Pollinator-aware conventional uses synthetic products but chooses them carefully and times applications to minimize pollinator impact. Most clients use the second approach.
Is this related to The Alleman Apiary?
Yes — same owner, same phone number. Lawns Plants & Pests handles property-level pest control and lawn care. The Alleman Apiary handles bees and honey, including pollinator hive placement for orchards and farms.
