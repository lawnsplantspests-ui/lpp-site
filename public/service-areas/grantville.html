What Grantville Properties Deal With

Grantville sits in eastern Dauphin County along I-81 with a mix of rural properties, the Hollywood Casino corridor, and surrounding farmland out toward Manada Hill. Rural-edge living drives most of the typical pest pressures. That mix creates a specific set of pest pressures:

  • Spotted lanternfly along the I-81 corridor — heavy pressure on tree of heaven
  • Yellow jackets and bald-faced hornets in barns, sheds, and older homes
  • Mosquitoes and ticks on properties bordering woodlots
  • Deer browse and groundhog damage on rural-edge properties
  • Agricultural pest pressure on surrounding farms
  • Aquatic vegetation on farm ponds throughout the area
