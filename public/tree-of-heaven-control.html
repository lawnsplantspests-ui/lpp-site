Why Cutting It Down Doesn't Work

Tree-of-heaven fights back when it's cut. The stump and the roots send up new sprouts, often dozens of them, sometimes many feet from the original trunk. A property that had one tree can have a thicket a year after someone takes a chainsaw to it.

The fix is to kill the roots, not just the trunk. That's what hack-and-squirt does.

How Hack-and-Squirt Works

  • Cuts through the bark. A hatchet makes downward cuts spaced around the trunk, just deep enough to reach the living layer under the bark.
  • A small, measured dose in each cut. A herbicide labeled for this use goes into the cuts, at the amount the label allows. No broadcast spraying, no drift onto the plants around it.
  • The tree carries it to the roots. Applied at the right time of year, the tree moves the herbicide down into its root system on its own, which is why it sprouts back far less than a cut tree.
  • It dies standing. Leaves wilt and drop over the following weeks. If a treated tree is near a house, driveway, path or fence, it should come down once it's dead. That's a job for a tree service, and we'll talk it through with you before we treat.

Timing matters. The best results come from mid to late summer, when the tree is pulling its sugars down to the roots. That's when we schedule this work. Small stems and seedlings get a different treatment than full-size trees, and we check back the next summer for any sprouts or stems that were missed.

Is It Tree-of-Heaven?

It gets mixed up with sumac and black walnut. A few things to look for:

  • Long compound leaves with many leaflets, often 1 to 3 feet long.
  • One or two small bumps (notches) near the base of each leaflet. Sumac and walnut don't have them.
  • Crushed leaves and broken twigs have a strong, unpleasant smell, often compared to burnt peanut butter.
  • Smooth, light gray bark, and clusters of tan, papery seeds hanging on female trees into winter.
  • Spotted lanternflies gathering on the trunk in late summer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I just cut the tree down?
You can, but it will almost always come back as a cluster of sprouts from the stump and roots. Killing the tree first, then cutting it once it's dead if it needs to come down, gives far fewer sprouts.
When is the best time to treat?
Mid to late summer. Call in spring or early summer to get on the schedule. Work done outside that window doesn't reach the roots as well.
Will it come back?
Hack-and-squirt sprouts back far less than cutting, but a big infestation usually needs a follow-up the next summer for missed stems and seedlings. We'll tell you up front if your site needs one.
Is it safe for the trees and plants around it?
The herbicide goes into cuts in the target tree's bark, not sprayed into the air, so nothing drifts onto nearby plants. We follow every label rule for the product we use, including distances from water.
How is it priced?
